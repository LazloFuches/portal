The Bread Factory Question

On the Uncertain Fate of Ottawa's Artists & Their Workshops

By R. Egert, for Hyperallergic

Nearly one hundred artists have for some years maintained their workshops in a converted bread factory in Ottawa. The building has now been sold to developers who propose towers and have made promises to the resident artists of reduced rates in the future. Whether the artists will be displaced, accommodated, or if the project will be postponed remains a matter of conjecture. Read the full dispatch in Hyperallergic.

Carol Bruns at White Columns

Masks, Totems & Archaic Effigies in New York

By R. Egert, for D'art International

A review of sculptor Carol Bruns and her exhibition of masks, totems, and distorted effigies at White Columns in New York. The works draw on traditions from West Africa to archaic Greece, yet manage to feel palpably contemporary. Our critic found Bruns' sculpture to be enigmatic and worth the visit. Read the full review in D'art International.
