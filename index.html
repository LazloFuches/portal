Three Attic Vases from a Private Collection

Geometric, Archaic & Classical Periods

750 - 400 B.C.

Three ancient Greek ceramic vessels spanning nearly four centuries of Attic production: a Geometric skyphos with water birds, a black-figure lip cup attributed to Tleson, and a white-ground funerary lekythos. Full provenance, condition reports, and scholarly documentation.
